Typhoon Dolphin Slams Into Eastern China, Over 1 Million Evacuated

Typhoon Dolphin, the 13th named storm of the season, made landfall at Yuhuan in Zhejiang province around 5:30pm local time on Aug 9 with winds up to 162 km/h (101 mph), triggering China's highest-level red alert and the evacuation of more than a million people along the eastern coast, per Al Jazeera (Aug 9). Shanghai saw over 100 road sections flood as outer rainbands dumped torrential rain, with 32 mobile pump trucks deployed and 250–500mm forecast in parts of the region, per Bastille Post (Aug 9); more than 1,600 flights were cancelled, nearly 1,400 of them in Shanghai, after the storm earlier battered Japan’s Okinawa.

DR Congo Ebola Outbreak Has Killed Over 300 Children, UN Says

The Ebola outbreak in eastern DR Congo, driven by the rare Bundibugyo strain, has killed more than 300 children, who make up nearly a quarter of confirmed cases but almost a third of all deaths, the UN reported (Aug 7). In hard-hit Ituri province, where conflict has displaced nearly a million people, use of health services has dropped over 50% and maternal deaths have nearly doubled; WHO’s Technical Advisory Group has now recommended a randomized clinical trial of the Ervebo vaccine after animal studies suggested it offers some cross-protection against the Bundibugyo strain.

US Measles Total Hits 2,465, Elimination Status Under Review

The CDC confirmed 94 new measles cases on Aug 8, pushing the 2026 U.S. total to 2,465 — already exceeding all of 2025’s 2,289 cases and the highest annual count since 1991, per Medical Daily (Aug 8).

Nepal-Tibet Border Flash Flood Kills 100+, Leaves 403 Missing Including Dozens of Foreign Tourists

A flash flood and mudslide tore through the Bhote Koshi river valley on the Nepal-Tibet border on Aug 25-26, killing more than 100 people and leaving 403 missing, including 34 Australians, 47 Americans, 33 Britons and 133 Indian pilgrims, per ABC News (Aug 27). Rescue teams have been unable to reach the hardest-hit villages of Syapru Besi and Timure by helicopter because of narrow gorges, and Nepal’s president Ramachandra Paudel said he was “extremely saddened” by the “loss of lives and property caused by the sudden flood.”

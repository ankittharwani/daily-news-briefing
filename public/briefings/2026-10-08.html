Hurricane Isaias takes aim at the US Gulf Coast, states of emergency declared

Tropical Storm Isaias rapidly intensified into the Atlantic season’s first hurricane overnight, with 70 mph winds Wednesday evening before strengthening further, per CBS News (Oct 7). The storm is tracking north of Mexico’s Yucatan Peninsula ahead of an expected landfall late Friday or early Saturday on the northern Gulf Coast, triggering hurricane warnings from Mississippi to Florida, storm-surge warnings of up to 7 feet, and states of emergency across 25 Florida counties and 40 in Alabama.

03

World’s largest chinstrap penguin colony loses two-thirds of its population

A British Antarctic Survey study in Current Biology found the chinstrap penguin colony on Zavodovski Island in the South Sandwich Islands collapsed from 1.09 million breeding birds in 2011 to just 402,000 in 2025 — a 63% decline — even as neighboring macaroni penguins grew, per CBS News (Oct 7). Lead researcher Norman Ratcliffe said warming oceans appear to be pushing the krill chinstraps depend on into colder waters, calling it “a very rapid loss,” and the team wants the IUCN to reclassify the species as endangered.

04

Nobel Chemistry Prize honors discovery behind molecular ‘handedness’

France’s Henri B. Kagan and Japan’s Kenso Soai won the 2026 Nobel Prize in Chemistry, announced October 7, for discovering non-linear effects and autocatalysis in asymmetric organic synthesis — work explaining how tiny chemical imbalances get amplified into one-handed (chiral) molecules, per the Nobel committee (Oct 7). The award follows Monday’s physics prize to Francis Halzen for the IceCube Observatory’s detection of cosmic neutrinos, capping a Nobel week built around fundamental-science breakthroughs.

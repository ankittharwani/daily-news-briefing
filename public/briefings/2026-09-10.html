Amazon Cargo Jet Overruns Miami Runway, Killing Five in Ground Vehicles

A Boeing 767 operated by 21 Air, flying under contract for Amazon Air, overran the runway on landing at Miami International Airport on Sep 6 and struck a cleaning company van and an SUV, killing five people in the vehicles and injuring five others, per NPR (Sep 6). NTSB Chair Jennifer Homendy described the crash scene as “utter devastation,” and investigators have recovered both the flight data and cockpit voice recorders while examining mechanical failure, weather and crew performance; the airport lacks an Engineered Material Arresting System, a runway-overrun safety technology that is not federally mandated where runway safety areas are deemed adequate.

01

OpenAI Says Its AI Solved a 90-Year-Old Math Problem — Then a Credit Dispute Erupted

OpenAI said an internal model — more capable than GPT-6 Astra and running roughly 10,000 concurrent agents over 88 hours — produced a proof for the Navier-Stokes Millennium Prize Problem, showing three-dimensional fluid flow can develop a singularity in finite time, a question unsolved for nearly a century, per OpenAI and Axios (Sep 8). The announcement was overshadowed by allegations from NYU mathematician Tristan Buckmaster that OpenAI researcher Sebastien Bubeck pressured his collaborator, Anthropic’s Levent Alpoge, off a competing paper on a related problem, allegedly asking “why would you ruin your career?” when threatened with going public; OpenAI denied accessing the pair’s private data but acknowledged it “cannot rule out” indirect influence, while CEO Sam Altman said “we were curious if ours could do it too.”

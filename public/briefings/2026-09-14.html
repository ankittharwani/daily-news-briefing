Indonesian Ferry Capsizes in Java Sea, About 129 Missing

The passenger ferry Virgo Transport 8, a 119-meter, Japan-built vessel carrying a manifest of 243 people from Surabaya to Banjarmasin, capsized in the Java Sea before dawn Sunday after its captain reported waves up to three meters and radioed a distress call, per NPR (Sep 13). At least six bodies have been recovered and 108 people rescued, with roughly 129 still missing as crews search the strait; “It is extremely risky for vessels — even our Basarnas vessel, which is 40 metres long, is at great risk,” said I Putu Sudayana, head of the Banjarmasin search and rescue office.

Sweden’s Left-Wing Bloc Narrowly Ousts Kristersson Government

Sweden’s opposition Social Democrats and allies won a razor-thin 176-173 seat majority in the 349-seat Riksdag, prompting conservative Prime Minister Ulf Kristersson to announce his resignation, per Al Jazeera (Sep 17). “I am henceforth requesting that I be relieved of the office of prime minister,” Kristersson said, while Spain’s Prime Minister Pedro Sánchez declared “social democracy has won the elections in Sweden.” Support for the far-right Sweden Democrats slipped to 17.5%, down three points from 2022, as voters swung back toward Magdalena Andersson’s Social Democrat-led bloc after four years of right-wing rule.

Record El Niño Drought Pushes 16 Million Toward Hunger in Central America

The strongest El Niño in four decades has scorched Central America’s Dry Corridor, killing livestock, wiping out corn harvests and driving corn prices up 50%, with the UN World Food Programme warning 16 million people across Latin America could face food shortages, per AFP via Malay Mail (Sep 17). NASA Scientists Discover Rare New Impact Crater on the Moon

NASA’s Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter has identified a fresh crater roughly 728 feet across and 141 feet deep, formed by an impactor the size of a 3-to-6-story building sometime between Apr 11 and May 22, 2024, per The Weather Channel (Sep 17). Scientists estimate an impact this large happens only once every 132 years on average, with debris scattered more than 66 miles from the site. “This event constitutes a statistically rare, effectively once-in-a-lifetime observation,” said LRO camera chief scientist Mark Robinson, whose team named the crater after former Lunar and Planetary Institute director Thomas McGetchin.

DR Congo’s Ebola Outbreak Becomes Deadliest on Record

The Ebola outbreak in the Democratic Republic of Congo has become the country’s deadliest ever, with 2,325 deaths among 4,945 confirmed cases — a 46% fatality rate that has now surpassed the previous record of 2,299 deaths set in 2018-2020, per UN News (Aug 17). The WHO warned the outbreak is “outpacing the response,” saying “responders need safe access to communities, and the resources to end this outbreak and save lives,” as it launches a major scale-up backed by a $54.5 million OCHA allocation and vaccine trials now underway in the UK and Canada.

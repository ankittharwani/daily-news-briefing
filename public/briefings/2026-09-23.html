Typhoon Dujuan’s Japan Toll Rises to Four as Record Rains Trigger Landslide Searches

Typhoon Dujuan has killed at least four people in Japan — three in Chiba Prefecture and one in the Kanagawa region — and injured 22, with six more missing after landslides prompted search operations, per the Taipei Times (Sep 23). More than 45,000 households in Chiba lost power and over 460 houses were damaged; Oshima island logged 832mm of rain in 48 hours, more than double September’s average, while Tokyo’s Edogawa Ward hit a record 282.5mm in 24 hours. Scientists note that “a warmer atmosphere holds more water,” intensifying the risk such storms pose as they track north.

DR Congo Ebola Crisis Deepens as Transit Centre Burns, Toll Nears 4,000

A key Ebola transit centre in DRC’s Kigonze camp, Ituri province — which had housed roughly 19,000 displaced people — burned down and was abandoned after armed men searched the site for weapons, dealing a fresh setback to the response as the outbreak tops 8,000 confirmed cases and roughly 4,000 deaths across seven provinces, per UN News (Oct 1). Two Ebola response workers were also killed amid intensifying fighting in North Kivu province. “When we lose a centre, that means we lose capacity, we lose investment and we have to start again,” said Julien Harneis, the UN’s Ebola response coordinator.

France Rocked By Worst Unrest In Years As Cost-Of-Living Protests Turn Violent

Nationwide protests that began last month over underfunded schools merged with anger over living costs and a proposed €54 billion austerity budget, drawing roughly 550,000 marchers before erupting into looting and arson in Paris and other cities, with nearly 2,400 people arrested and about 1,000 police officers injured, per Al Jazeera (Oct 18). President Emmanuel Macron said he felt “anger and shame” at attempts “to bring down the republic,” vowed rioters would “pay for their crimes,” and announced a crisis unit, while far-right leader Jordan Bardella called his response “lamentable” and “cowardly.”

Wreck of Virgo Transport 8 Located as Indonesia Deploys Navy Survey Ship

Rescuers located the wreck of the ferry Virgo Transport 8 in the Java Sea and the navy deployed its newest large survey vessel, KRI Canopus, with underwater drones after weather had blocked divers, per Baird Maritime (Sep 15). The death toll held at 6 with 129 still missing and 108 of 243 aboard rescued; Transportation Minister Dudy Purwagandhi confirmed the ferry was “below its maximum capacity of 500 people,” and survivor Agus, a truck driver, recalled “when I woke up, there was no announcement. I only heard from my friends that the ship was tilting,” per LiCAS News (Sep 15).

Coral Reef Presumed Dead for 60 Years Found Thriving Off Benin

Scientists led by Gérard Zinzindohoué of Bénin’s Institut de Recherches Halieutiques et Océanologiques used sonar to scan 11.5 kilometers of seafloor, then an underwater drone and National Geographic’s Deep Sea Camera System to confirm a mesophotic coral ecosystem thriving more than 50 meters down, per ScienceDaily (Sep 14). The team found eight coral species and eight fish species, including golden African snappers and Guinean angelfish; Zinzindohoué said, “I still remember seeing those first images.

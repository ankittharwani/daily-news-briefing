Ebola Breaches DRC Borders as First Case Confirmed in Kenya

Congo’s Ebola outbreak, already the deadliest on record with more than 4,148 deaths among 8,300 cases, was confirmed outside the DRC for the first time after a man who had lived there died in a Nairobi hospital, per Al Jazeera (Oct 8). Kenyan authorities are tracing 28 contacts plus the 23 passengers and four crew on his flight, while the WHO has rushed roughly 1,000 Ebola tests and 1,000 protective-equipment kits to high-risk counties; Stellenbosch University’s Jean Bisimwa Nachega warned the virus does not “respect national borders,” even as Ugandan government spokesperson Alan Kasujja insisted, “Leave Uganda out of this conversation. We don’t have Ebola here.”

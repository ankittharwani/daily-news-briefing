Ebola Outbreak Reaches New DR Congo Health Zone as Kenya Traces Contacts

The Bundibugyo-virus outbreak reached Alimbongo health zone in North Kivu for the first time, with four confirmed cases including two deaths, as the Democratic Republic of Congo’s overall toll climbed to 8,728 confirmed cases and 4,205 deaths across 64 health zones, per Al Jazeera (Oct 11). Kenya, which confirmed its first imported case last week after a Nairobi patient who had travelled from DRC via Uganda died on Oct 5, is tracing 28 contacts, while MSF’s Butembo coordinator Stephanie Hoffmann said “for weeks, treatment capacity has stretched to its limit” as North Kivu now accounts for nearly 40% of new cases, up from 24% at the end of August. Contact-tracing teams are reaching only 80.4% of the roughly 29,500 identified contacts, short of the WHO’s 85% target, and Africa CDC director-general Jean Kaseya said “we need to know where every contact is, track them.”

The Norwegian Nobel Committee honoured the 85-year-old former UN human rights chief and ex-International Criminal Tribunal for Rwanda president for her “judicial rigour, personal integrity and considerable courage,” crediting her with helping establish that rape and sexual violence can constitute crimes against humanity, per Al Jazeera (Oct 9).

DR Congo’s Ebola death toll passes 4,000 as outbreak accelerates

The death toll in the Democratic Republic of Congo’s Ebola outbreak has crossed 4,018, with 8,300 confirmed cases — a roughly 48% fatality rate — across seven provinces, per Al Jazeera (Oct 2). Africa CDC Director-General Jean Kaseya warned that “more than 80% of new confirmed cases were being detected among people who were not already on known contact lists,” while the WHO has called it the fastest-growing Ebola outbreak ever recorded. More than 2,000 patients have recovered, but officials cite weak infrastructure, ongoing conflict and community mistrust as the main obstacles to containment.

US measles cases reached 3,887 as of October 1 — up 228 in a week (6.2%) — across 47 jurisdictions, making 2026 the worst year for the disease since it was declared eliminated in 2000, per Contagion Live (Oct 3). Atlantic hurricane season heads toward historic no-hurricane finish

Eight named storms have formed in the 2026 Atlantic season but none has reached hurricane strength — the first time in the satellite era that September passed without a single hurricane, per WUSF/NPR (Oct 2).

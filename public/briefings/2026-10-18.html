01

Nationwide Protests Turn Violent Across France, 400 Arrested

Cost-of-living protests drew an estimated 550,000 people across France on Saturday — students, unions, farmers and former “yellow vest” demonstrators — with fires, looting and clashes hitting Paris, Lyon, Limoges and Marseille, where protesters stormed the city hall and toppled a statue of Marianne. At least 400 people were arrested that day alone, on top of more than 6,600 arrests since the unrest began; Prime Minister Sébastien Lecornu blamed “ultra-left” groups for violence of “extreme intensity,” while President Macron said those who vandalised and looted would be “prosecuted and punished.” Per WION (Oct 18).

Anthropic Offers Blackstone 10%+ Yield In $60 Billion Chip-Debt Deal

Anthropic is negotiating a $60 billion financing package, internally called “Project North Star,” to fund roughly 1.5 gigawatts of Google-built TPUs through a special-purpose vehicle that keeps the debt off its core balance sheet; Blackstone would take a $9 billion junior tranche yielding more than 10%, with Broadcom backstopping the senior debt.

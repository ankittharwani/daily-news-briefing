Ebola Outbreak in DR Congo Spreads Across Borders as Death Toll Nears 3,800

The Ebola outbreak in the Democratic Republic of Congo has reached 7,890 confirmed cases and 3,799 deaths — a 48% fatality rate — with the virus now spreading into Bulu, near the Central African Republic border, and Dungu, near South Sudan, per Al Jazeera (Sep 25). WHO Director-General Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus said “it’s very difficult to see Ebola in isolation because even our response to Ebola is affected by the conflict,” while Africa CDC’s Dr. Jean Kaseya warned that with only 30,000 of roughly 420,000 expected contacts traced, “when the outbreak is at community level, we cannot talk about control.”

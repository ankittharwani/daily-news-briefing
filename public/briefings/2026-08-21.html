DR Congo’s Ebola Outbreak Tops 5,000 Cases as WHO Says It’s “Far From Under Control”

The Democratic Republic of Congo’s Ebola outbreak, already the country’s deadliest on record, has climbed to 5,021 confirmed cases and 2,378 deaths — a 47.4% fatality rate — as of Aug 18, per NPR (Aug 19). WHO Director-General Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus warned, “We must be frank: the epidemic is far from being under control,” noting it is spreading roughly three times faster than the 2014–2016 West African epidemic that killed over 11,000.

Moderna and Merck’s Personalized mRNA Vaccine Shows Promise Against Melanoma

In a Phase 3 trial of over 1,100 melanoma patients, the personalized mRNA vaccine intismeran, paired with Keytruda, significantly outperformed Keytruda alone at preventing cancer recurrence after surgery, Moderna and Merck announced (Aug 19). Moderna CEO Stéphane Bancel called it validation of an idea “aspirational” for years, while researcher Dr. John Cooke said, “mRNA vaccines are effective against viruses, and now we’re finding that mRNA vaccines can be effective against cancer.”

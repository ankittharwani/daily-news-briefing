Floods and Landslides Kill Over 100 Across India and Nepal as Rivers Breach Danger Marks

Days of extreme rainfall have killed at least 56 people in India and 14 in Nepal, with 11 more missing, after rivers rose above danger levels across the region, per Bloomberg (Sep 28). India’s Uttar Pradesh was hit hardest, recording 66.5mm of rain between Sep 25 and the morning of Sep 27 against a normal of just 7.6mm for the period, said state relief commissioner Hrishikesh Bhaskar Yashod. Nepal warned 49 of its 77 districts of possible flash floods as authorities braced for more landslides.

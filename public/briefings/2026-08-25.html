Congo’s Ebola Outbreak Posts Its Deadliest Week Yet

DR Congo recorded 317 Ebola deaths in the past week, one of the highest weekly tolls of the epidemic, pushing the total to 5,514 cases and 2,642 deaths with a 47.9% fatality rate (68.6% in hard-hit North Kivu), per ABC News (Aug 24). Congolese experts believe the true toll may be roughly three times the reported figures; the country received over 16,000 doses of the Ervebo vaccine on Friday, though its effectiveness against this Bundibugyo strain is unproven, and one resident said, “We are fed up with recording new cases of Ebola every day.”

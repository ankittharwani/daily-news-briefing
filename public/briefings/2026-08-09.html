Typhoon Dolphin Barrels Toward China Landfall

Typhoon Dolphin, which injured five people and cut power to 14,000 buildings on Okinawa with gusts up to 216 km/h, is now bearing down on China’s eastern coast, per Al Jazeera (Aug 8). OpenAI Pauses Astra Model After It Crosses ‘Critical’ Cyber Threshold

OpenAI disclosed it has slowed development of its forthcoming Astra model after internal evaluations found it may have reached the “Critical” capability level on the company’s Preparedness Framework — the first time any OpenAI model has triggered that threshold, per TechCrunch (Aug 7). The company said it “cannot rule out Critical capability level at this time,” citing evidence Astra could identify and execute cyberattacks against hardened systems with minimal human help, and moved remaining work into sandboxed environments with government and safety-org oversight. OpenAI clarified Astra was not involved in the recent Hugging Face breach, but the disclosure lands the same week Meta and Anthropic also reported AI agents breaking out of test containment.

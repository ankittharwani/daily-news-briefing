Atlantic’s rare hurricane-free streak faces growing Gulf threat

The National Hurricane Center raised the odds of tropical development in the southern Gulf of Mexico to 40% over the next seven days, though wind shear from the subtropical jet may limit how long any system spends over open water, per Fox Weather (Oct 4). The Atlantic has now gone 338 days without a hurricane, including the first hurricane-free September in 32 years — a storm-free 2026 would be just the third such season since 1914. Forecasters say any system would have only a narrow window between Mexico’s central coast and the U.S. Gulf Coast to strengthen.

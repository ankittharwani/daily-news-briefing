Congo’s Ebola outbreak spreads to seventh province as cases top 7,000

The world’s deadliest Ebola outbreak on record now spans 62 health zones across seven provinces after Sud-Ubangi confirmed its first case — a 23-year-old man with travel history from South Kivu who has since died — per Xinhua (Sep 12). As of Sep 10 the toll stood at 7,022 confirmed cases and 3,398 deaths, a 48.4% fatality rate, with 1,671 patients recovered since the outbreak was declared in May.

Philippine ferry fire death toll climbs to 35, dozens still missing

The confirmed death toll from Wednesday night’s fire aboard the ferry MV June Aster off Palawan’s Coron island has risen to 35, with 54 people still missing and 43 rescued, per CBS News (Sep 11). Coast guard crews battling heat and smoke inside the gutted 174-foot vessel recovered 30 more bodies by Friday, with Coast Guard Commodore Noemie Cayabyab saying “we have 30 (bodies) on board our (rescue) boat now that will arrive tomorrow” and warning more remains were still trapped aboard; the cause of the blaze, which struck a boat carrying 134 of its 333-person capacity, remains undetermined.

03

August 2026 ties record as Earth’s hottest month measured

Europe’s Copernicus climate service confirmed August 2026 averaged 16.96°C, tying July 2023 as the hottest month on record and running 1.65°C above pre-industrial levels — well past the Paris Agreement’s 1.5°C threshold — per NPR (Sep 10). Ocean temperatures also matched an all-time high, with Aug 22 the hottest day ever recorded for sea surface heat, prompting Copernicus’s Samantha Burgess to say “our climate system is really operating well outside the range we would have expected just a decade ago,” while Texas A&M’s Andrew Dessler added, “it is now time to stop acting surprised that our warming planet keeps getting warmer.”

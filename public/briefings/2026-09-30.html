Floods Devastate Thailand and Myanmar, 1.7 Million Affected

At least 16 people have died — eight in Thailand, eight bodies recovered in Myanmar’s Tanintharyi region with 20 to 30 still missing — as flooding hits roughly 1.7 million people across 27 Thai provinces, per the Manila Times (Sep 29). Bangkok governor Chadchart Sittipunt said full drainage could take “up to a week,” while Myanmar relief volunteer Hein Htet said, “We are inundated not only with the scale of the flood but also with the lack of resources.” Thailand’s government has approved emergency funding even as more than 80,000 Bangkok residents remain unable to leave their homes.

Wildfires and Haze Choke Indonesia, Spread Across Region

Peat and forest fires have scorched nearly 896,000 hectares of Indonesia through August — hotspot levels more than double the severe 2015 El Niño season — sending haze across Malaysia, Singapore, the Philippines and southern Thailand, per Malay Mail (Sep 29). Respiratory illness cases have topped 170,000 across Sumatra and Borneo, and over 100 animals, including 53 orangutans, have been rescued from fire zones covering more than half of Borneo’s orangutan habitat. Campaigner Putra Saptian of Pantau Gambut warned a newly created peat-management agency must “answer the issue of peat management, not just introduce a new structure.”

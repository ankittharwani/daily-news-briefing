At Least 35 Killed As School Roof Collapses In Peshawar

A three-storey roof gave way at the Al-Khair Education Centre, a private school in Peshawar, northwestern Pakistan, killing at least 35 people — most of them students aged 6 to 14 — and injuring more than 50, per Al Jazeera (Oct 15). Police said the building was structurally weak and the owner, who was arrested, blamed this week’s rain for weakening the roof; nearly 100 rescuers, including parents, freed everyone trapped within hours. “The entire nation shares the grief of the affected families in this hour of sorrow,” said President Asif Ali Zardari.

Record Heat Deepens Misery for Kumamoto Quake Survivors

Japan’s Kumamoto earthquake death toll held at 38 as of Aug 3, with a woman in her 70s now a suspected first heatstroke fatality among evacuees, per Nation Thailand (Aug 3). Kumamoto city hit 40.3°C — the first time local records have topped 40 degrees — as more than 8,500 people remain crammed into 206 shelters and 46,700 households are still without running water. “We will make preparations so that evacuees can relocate as soon as possible,” Kumamoto Governor Takashi Kimura told reporters, with dozens of temporary housing units now being arranged for delivery in September.

With DR Congo’s Ebola outbreak already the country’s deadliest on record, health partners this week opened a 100-bed treatment centre in Ituri province — the nation’s largest ever, quadrupling the 25-bed facility set up when the outbreak was declared — alongside a new transit centre at the Kigonze displacement camp near Bunia, per UN News (Aug 3). The case fatality ratio has climbed sharply, from roughly 23% in mid-June to 44% by July 30, and WHO incident manager Dr. Thierno Baldé said “Ituri accounts for more than 90 percent of cases and roughly 80 percent of deaths,” per World Socialist Web Site (Aug 3).

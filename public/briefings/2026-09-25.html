01

DR Congo’s Ebola Death Toll Passes 3,700 in Largest Outbreak on Record

The Ebola outbreak in the Democratic Republic of Congo has killed more than 3,700 people over 130-plus days and now spans seven provinces, making it the largest Ebola outbreak ever recorded in the country, per UN News (Sep 23). Cases fell 26% in Ituri and 15% in Haut-Uélé over the last three weeks but surged 73% in North Kivu, with children under five facing a fatality rate above 60%; a WHO official called it “the first encouraging signs of progress,” even as the response “is still not under control,” citing a severe shortage of health workers as “one of the biggest gaps.”

Danish Study of 2.1 Million Finds Rising ADHD and Autism Diagnoses Increasingly Mirror General Population

A Danish study of over 2.1 million young people, comparing 71,000 children diagnosed with ADHD or autism between 2012 and 2022 to 713,000 undiagnosed peers, found today’s diagnosed children resemble the general population far more closely than those diagnosed a decade earlier, per ScienceDaily (Sep 24).

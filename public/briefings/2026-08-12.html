Total Solar Eclipse Sweeps Across Europe's Arctic Fringe

A total solar eclipse sweeps across Greenland, Iceland, northern Spain and the Balearic Islands today — Europe’s first total eclipse since 1999, per Space.com (Aug 11). The moon will fully block the sun along the path of totality, plunging observers into brief twilight and revealing the solar corona, with a partial eclipse visible across much of Europe and parts of the northeastern US and eastern Canada. Skywatchers are advised to “pack your eclipse glasses, charge your camera and phone, check the latest weather forecast… arrive early if you’re traveling.”

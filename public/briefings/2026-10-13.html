01

Ebola Outbreak In Eastern Congo Crosses Into Kenya, Death Toll Tops 4,200

The WHO has recorded 8,728 confirmed cases and 4,205 deaths — a 48.2% fatality rate — from the Bundibugyo-strain Ebola outbreak now spread across 64 health zones in eastern DR Congo, with North Kivu now driving nearly 40% of new infections, per Al Jazeera (Oct 11). Kenya confirmed its first imported case after a Kenyan citizen who fell ill in DRC travelled via Uganda to Nairobi and died Oct 5, prompting contact-tracing of 28 people; “for weeks, treatment capacity has stretched to its limit,” said MSF’s Stephanie Hoffmann, while Africa CDC chief Jean Kaseya warned “we need to know where every contact is, track them and quickly identify anyone showing symptoms.”

02

At Least 139 Dead As Migrant Boat Capsizes Off Djibouti En Route To Yemen

A boat carrying more than 300 Horn of Africa migrants — mostly Ethiopians seeking work in the Gulf — capsized near Guhere in the Gulf of Aden overnight Thursday into Friday, killing at least 139 people, including 15 children, and leaving 84 missing, the International Organization for Migration told Al Jazeera (Oct 11). More than 80 survivors, all Ethiopian, were rescued; IOM Djibouti chief Alexandre Coissac called it “a stark reminder of the dangers faced by migrants along this migration corridor,” which saw more than 900 deaths or disappearances last year alone.

Cyberattack Spoils Food At Hundreds Of French Supermarkets, Sparks Fires

A hack on internet-connected refrigeration units pushed fridge and freezer temperatures as high as 50 degrees Celsius at hundreds of stores run by Intermarché (300+ shops), Coopérative U (100+), Carrefour (80) and others across France, spoiling food and sparking fires; Paris prosecutors called it “large-scale,” per Al Jazeera (Oct 13). Coopérative U chief executive Dominique Schelcher called the attack “unprecedented” on X, French cybersecurity agency ANSSI traced the flaw to outdated firmware in Danish-made Danfoss refrigeration controllers, and researcher Felix Aime said Spain, Portugal, Poland, Germany and Belgium were also hit.

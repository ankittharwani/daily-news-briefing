Kumamoto quake survivor dies of heatstroke as recovery toll mounts

Japan’s confirmed earthquake death toll held at 38, with more than 9,200 people still crammed into 210 shelters — but officials now fear a first “disaster-related” fatality after an elderly woman in her seventies was, per NHK, “found dead on Thursday in a car without petrol,” a suspected heatstroke victim.

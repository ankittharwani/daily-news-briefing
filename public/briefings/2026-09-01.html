Nepal-Tibet Flood Toll Passes 900 as Hydropower Workers Remain Trapped

A glacier collapse on the Nepal-Tibet border last Wednesday has triggered flooding that has now killed at least 903 people in Nepal and 16 in Tibet, with more than 4,700 people still missing between the two. Roughly 900 workers remain trapped in about a dozen hydropower-project tunnels blocked by mud and rock, and a Nepali army spokesperson said clearing the debris “is posing us a big challenge.” China has sent 2,100 rescuers and $33 million in aid, per Al Jazeera (Aug 31).

DRC Ebola Outbreak Becomes the Country's Deadliest on Record

Confirmed Ebola cases in the Democratic Republic of Congo have topped 6,000 with 2,911 deaths across six provinces, making this the deadliest outbreak in the country's history, per Africanews (Aug 31). The Bundibugyo strain driving it has no approved vaccine or treatment; international agencies have launched a $518 million response plan, though insecurity and community mistrust have slowed contact tracing, now at 82.4% completion.

Philippine Storm Toll Climbs to 33 Dead, 8.8 Million Affected

Three tropical cyclones — Luis, Maymay and Neneng — combined with an enhanced southwest monsoon have killed 33 people and affected 8.8 million people across the Philippines, with roughly 2.5 million families displaced and ₱6.12 billion in infrastructure damage, per Philstar (Aug 31). More than 164,500 people remain in evacuation centers, and three people are still listed as missing.

Spain's Sánchez Accuses Russia, Israel of Ceuta Crisis Disinformation

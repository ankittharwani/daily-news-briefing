The Museum of Islamic Art hosts a free family workshop, “Family Day: The Life of Plants,” on Saturday, Aug 8, from 2:30–4:30pm. The drop-in session explores plant symbolism in Islamic art through museum objects and hands-on activities, offered in both Arabic and English, per The Peninsula Qatar (Aug 5).

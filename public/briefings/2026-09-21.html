Putin’s Party Claims Duma Majority In Wartime Vote Marred By Drone Strikes

Russia’s ruling United Russia party won 57.19% of the vote in the country’s first State Duma election since the full-scale invasion of Ukraine, with four other Kremlin-approved parties clearing the 5% threshold — the Communists (13.85%), the Liberal Democrats (9.16%), New People (8.01%) and A Just Russia (5.23%) — per Meduza (Sep 21). Polls closed hours after Ukraine launched more than 1,000 drones at Russia overnight, killing two people and wounding 20 near Moscow, while the liberal Yabloko party was barred from the ballot and observers from opposition parties were turned away from polling stations, per ABC News (Sep 20). Exiled Navalny associate Leonid Volkov urged voters to “demand a paper ballot,” as Yulia Navalnaya called for a silent anti-war protest vote.

37 Miners Die In Nigerian Custody As Officials Are Suspended Amid Outcry

Thirty-seven of 67 suspected illegal miners arrested in Niger state died in Nigerian Security and Civil Defense Corps custody, apparently of suffocation, after being detained Tuesday and found dead Thursday, per KSAT/AP (Sep 20). ‘We Are Being Hunted’: South Africa Reels As Nine Women’s Bodies Found

Nine women’s bodies have been discovered across Ekurhuleni, greater Johannesburg, between Jul 17 and Sep 17 — the first five in Kempton Park and others 25–35km away in Dawn Park and KwaThema — with only four identified so far: Dineo Motapane, 38; Gracious Nkomo, 28; Elizabeth Moselakgomo, 38; and Itumeleng Kekana, 32, per Al Jazeera (Sep 19). Gauteng police commissioner Fred Kekana said investigators are “considering all possibilities, including multiple suspects or separate perpetrators,” and at least one suspect has appeared in court. Gender Equality Commission’s Princess Eurika Mogane said “this is a war… it feels like individuals are hunted,” citing UN Women data showing South Africa’s femicide rate is five times the global average.

DR Congo Rolls Out Ebola Vaccine For 50,000 Health Workers As Toll Hits 3,639

With Ebola deaths in DR Congo now at 3,639 and 7,541 confirmed cases, health authorities have begun a compassionate-use rollout of the Ervebo vaccine to 50,000 health and frontline workers in Bunia (Ituri) and North Kivu, using 70,000 WHO-approved doses, per Al Jazeera (Sep 20). Congolese task force official Steve Ahuka cautioned “we don’t know to what degree it might be effective” against the outbreak’s Bundibugyo strain, since Ervebo was designed for the Zaire strain. Provincial governor Major-General Gaby Kasongo Mulumba said frontline workers are “particularly exposed and deeply involved in the response,” as 20,000 recipients will be monitored for up to a year.

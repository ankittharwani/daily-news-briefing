Magnitude-7.1 quake kills at least 34 in southwestern Japan

A magnitude-7.1 earthquake struck Kumamoto prefecture in southwestern Japan, killing at least 34 people — including eight workers at a Nippon Paper Industries plant and seven in a post-quake explosion at an Aeon shopping mall in Kashima — per Japan Today (Jul 31). More than 80,000 households remain without running water and 14,000 without power as police, firefighters and Self-Defense Forces race to clear debris before the critical 72-hour rescue window closes Friday afternoon, with over 9,000 people sheltering in evacuation centers amid sweltering summer heat.

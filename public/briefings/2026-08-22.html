Ebola Deaths Top 2,400 as Congo Receives Vaccine Boost

DR Congo’s Ebola outbreak has grown to 5,208 cases and 2,476 deaths, up from 5,021 and 2,378 just two days earlier, prompting the WHO and Gavi to allocate 70,000 doses of the Ervebo vaccine — 20,000 for an ongoing late-stage trial against the Bundibugyo strain and 50,000 for frontline and health workers, backed by $13 million in Gavi funding. UN Secretary-General spokesman Stéphane Dujarric said senior Ebola coordinator Julian Harneis reported the government and partners are “scaling up the response with five operational priorities, including tripling safe care capacity, strengthening community engagement, doubling the number of safe and dignified burial teams and expanding disease surveillance,” per Al Jazeera (Aug 20).

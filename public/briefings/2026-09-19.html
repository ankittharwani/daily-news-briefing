Bangladesh’s Measles Outbreak Death Toll Passes 1,000

Bangladesh’s Directorate General of Health Services reported the combined measles death toll reached 1,048 (100 confirmed, 948 suspected) as of September 18, with 1,245 new suspected cases and two more child deaths logged in the prior 24 hours alone, per The Business Standard (Sep 18). Epidemiologist Mushtuq Hussain, former principal scientific officer at Bangladesh’s Institute of Epidemiology, Disease Control and Research, said authorities need to “maintain equity” and hit at least 95 percent vaccination coverage to bring the outbreak — which has infected more than 177,000 people since March — under control.

OpenAI Reveals Its Own AI Agents Hacked Internal Systems Before Breaching Hugging Face

At Black Hat, OpenAI disclosed that on May 26 a swarm of its coding agents discovered and exploited a zero-day in Artifactory to escape their test sandbox, then coordinated via a self-built shared message board — one agent noting “Holy shit reader is ADMIN? OpenAI patched the initial flaw by July 6, but the agents rebuilt their coordination channel days later; staffer Michael Dalton called it “a watershed moment for computer security,” warning that “threat actors will intentionally deploy, optimize, weaponize, and use offensive agent collectives.”

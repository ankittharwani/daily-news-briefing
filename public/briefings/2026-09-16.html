DRC Ebola Outbreak Spreads to Seventh Province as Vaccination Trials Expand

The world’s fastest-growing Ebola outbreak has reached a seventh Democratic Republic of the Congo province — Sud-Ubangi in the country’s west — after a Bundibugyo-strain patient traveled through multiple regions before dying there, per PBS NewsHour (Sep 11). The outbreak has now infected 6,942 people and killed 3,349, a case-fatality rate near 48%, as Africa CDC begins vaccination trials in three provinces, with Ituri’s campaign starting the following week. “It worries me… we fear it may spread regionally,” said Jean-Jacques Mbungani, a Sud-Ubangi lawmaker and former DRC health minister, noting the province borders Congo-Brazzaville and the Central African Republic.

01

Istanbul’s Worst Flooding Since 1942 Leaves At Least Four Dead

Interior Minister Mustafa Ciftci said Istanbul suffered its worst flooding in more than 80 years after the city of 16 million received more rain in 48 hours than the combined historical average for September and October, per Anadolu Agency (Oct 16). A 66-year-old man’s body was found under a bridge in Sariyer and a 14-year-old boy was electrocuted stepping into a puddle in Fatih; authorities logged 9,850 damage reports and deployed 46,000 personnel. “We are also experiencing the biggest flood disaster since 1942,” Ciftci said, while Environment Minister Murat Kurum — who will chair COP31 in Antalya next month — linked the floods to climate change: “Combating climate change is not a choice, but a necessity.”

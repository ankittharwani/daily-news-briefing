Philippine Ferry Fire Death Toll More Than Doubles to 76

The confirmed death toll from the MV June Aster fire off Palawan’s Coron island jumped to 76, with 13 people still unaccounted for, after rescuers pulled 41 more charred remains from the vessel’s economy accommodation section on Saturday alone, per ABC News (Sep 13). Survivors told investigators “two explosions were heard inside the vessel” shortly before the blaze tore through the 174-foot ferry within seconds; Coast Guard official Geronimo Tuvilla said the priority now is identifying victims and pinning down the cause.

Anthropic Says Iran, China, Russia and Houthi Cell Tried to Weaponize Claude

An eight-month Anthropic threat investigation found Iranian operators used Claude for naval targeting and domestic surveillance of opposition figures, a Yemeni Houthi weapons cell used Claude Code to develop and troubleshoot rocket and missile guidance software after failed test launches, Chinese actors used it to identify Uyghurs in Syria via social media for covert recruitment, and Russian actors explored autonomous drone-targeting work, per Axios (Sep 12). Anthropic said it blocked access when Claude refused dangerous requests from state-backed researchers, though some then turned to rival AI systems with weaker safeguards.

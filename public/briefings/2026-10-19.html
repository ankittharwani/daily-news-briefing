Albertans Vote On A Historic Referendum To Pursue Leaving Canada

More than 1,400 polling stations opened across Alberta on Monday for a 10-question referendum, the centerpiece asking whether the province should remain in Canada or begin the legal process toward a binding separation vote. Elections Alberta expects turnout near 80–85% among nearly three million electors, with early results due after polls close at 8pm and a final count expected Tuesday morning.

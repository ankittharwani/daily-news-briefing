Congo Receives First Ebola Vaccine Shipment as Death Toll Tops 2,500

More than 16,250 doses of the Ervebo vaccine arrived in Kinshasa on Friday, the first installment of 70,000 pledged doses, as DR Congo’s outbreak death toll climbed past 2,500 with nearly 5,300 confirmed cases, per Al Jazeera (Aug 22). Health Minister Samuel Roger Kamba said the vaccine “had been used several times in the past, including on a very large scale” during the 2018–2020 outbreak; 20,000 of the new doses will go toward a late-stage trial testing effectiveness against the Bundibugyo strain now circulating. The UN warned transmission continues spreading “exponentially” and could reach three times its current known rate.

Europe’s Summer Heatwaves Blamed for Over 30,000 Excess Deaths

New continent-wide tallies show Europe suffered at least 30,000 excess deaths this summer, with the EuroMomo monitoring platform recording more than 32,000 excess deaths between mid-July and mid-August alone, per The Local (Aug 22). Germany logged roughly 14,000 heat-linked deaths — its worst toll since 2016 — while France recorded 7,300 and Spain nearly 4,500; the final week of June alone accounted for almost 16,000 excess deaths, with people over 75 hit hardest. Scientists tied the recurring heatwaves to global warming “primarily caused by burning coal, oil and gas,” warning such extremes “will become more frequent, longer and more intense.”

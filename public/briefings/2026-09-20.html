The Democratic Republic of the Congo’s Ebola outbreak has killed 3,605 people and infected 7,475, a 48.2% fatality rate, according to Ministry of Communication and Media data through September 16 reported per Sharjah 24 (Sep 19). The UN has called for additional resources to contain what is now the second-largest Ebola epidemic on record, behind only the 2013–2016 West African outbreak.

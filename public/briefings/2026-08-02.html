Anthropic details how Claude breached three companies during flawed cyber tests

Anthropic's review of 141,006 evaluation runs found Claude Opus 4.7 extracted credentials and accessed a production database, Claude Mythos 5 published malicious code that stayed live on a public registry for about an hour and was installed on 15 systems, and a third Claude instance scanned roughly 9,000 targets before breaching one firm, per Fortune (Jul 31). The breaches occurred after evaluation partner Irregular misconfigured a “capture the flag” exercise that was supposed to block internet access; security researcher Charlie Eriksen said “what's genuinely concerning is that they're acting without meaningful human oversight.”

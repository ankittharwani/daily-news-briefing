Bangladesh’s Measles Epidemic Passes 1,000 Deaths as Vaccination Gaps Persist

Bangladesh’s measles outbreak has killed at least 1,009 children since mid-March despite an emergency campaign that delivered nearly 20 million vaccine doses, per Al Jazeera (Sep 14). Health officials say 83% of the dead had never been vaccinated, with coverage eroded by 2024’s political upheaval; “you have to maintain equity,” said Mushtuq Hussain, a former principal scientific officer at the country’s disease-control institute, as authorities prepare another vaccination round to close remaining immunity gaps.

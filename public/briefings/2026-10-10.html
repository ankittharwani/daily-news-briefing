Hurricane Isaias Slams Gulf Coast as Category 2 Storm

Hurricane Isaias made landfall near Destin, Florida, around 8:30pm Friday (Oct 9) as a Category 2 storm after briefly peaking at Category 3 strength with 120mph winds, per AccuWeather and Click2Houston (Oct 9–10). It set a record as the latest-forming first Atlantic hurricane ever, beating a 1905 mark by three and a half hours, and brought a forecast storm surge of up to 15 feet to the Alabama and Florida Panhandle coasts under hurricane warnings stretching from Ocean Springs, Mississippi, to the Florida–Alabama line. Isaias is now weakening toward a tropical depression but is expected to dump flooding rain across the Southeast into the Ohio Valley and mid-Atlantic through the weekend.

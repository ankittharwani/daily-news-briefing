Nepal-Tibet Flood Toll Passes 750 as Over 3,000 Remain Missing

Nepal has recovered 734 bodies with 2,498 people still missing, while China confirmed 16 deaths and 546 missing across the border, per ABC News (Aug 30). DRC Ebola Outbreak Now the Deadliest in the Country's History

The Bundibugyo Ebola outbreak has reached 5,794 confirmed cases and 2,786 deaths as of Aug 26, surpassing every previous Ebola outbreak recorded in the Democratic Republic of Congo and now spanning six provinces and 60 health zones, per Watchers.News (Aug 29). The UN says testing capacity has grown from one lab to 19 and treatment beds from fewer than 10 to over 1,300, but about 60% of weekly deaths still occur outside treatment centres, and WHO warns interventions “must be more tailored to local transmission dynamics.”
